# Grill Me

Do not activate implicitly. First state the current understanding, assumptions, constraints, and unresolved decisions; ask the user to correct that shared frame.

After agreement, challenge one decision at a time across goals, scope, alternatives, dependencies, failure modes, security, reversibility, evidence, and acceptance criteria. Keep questions concrete and adapt each next question to the answer. Distinguish blocking gaps from improvements that can be closed autonomously.

When the design is coherent, summarize the resolved design and obtain confirmation. Then require an independent-context Brainstorming review that receives the complete design and acceptance criteria but not the interrogation transcript or hidden reasoning. Reconcile any material discrepancy before implementation.

Never treat pressure testing as implementation, user authorization, test evidence, or review evidence. Preserve unresolved items and `unverified` capabilities explicitly.
